Nominated + publication Rijnstreek Business

  • 17 February 2020
  • Main news

BEGE has been nominated for ‘De Sterkste Schakel’ (the Strongest Link) 2020 and Rijnstreek Business came to visit for a nice publication

Once a year, the prestigious enterprise prize “The Strongest Link Trophy” is awarded to the most customer-oriented company in the Rhineland region. In 2020 this election will be organized for the seventeenth time. The election puts companies in the spotlight that elevate service and customer focus into art.

Customer focus in optima forma

The special thing about this election is that a company must be nominated to participate in the election. From the nominations by nominees from the previous election and jury members, only 25% is actually nominated. At BEGE we build long-term relationships and focus on our customers. Being flexible and personal service are central at BEGE. Already for 70 years. That BEGE is recognized by our own relations is what makes us so happy to make this known.

Strongest Link

The election is divided into 8 sectors. BEGE has been placed in “Construction, Installation & Technology”. The winner is selected per sector from all nominations. The sector winners and the final overall winner will be chosen by an expert jury (representatives of sector organizations and winners of previous editions). The Grand Final will take place next month. From March 7th ‘till March 13th you can vote for us. We’ll share the in the first week of March.
