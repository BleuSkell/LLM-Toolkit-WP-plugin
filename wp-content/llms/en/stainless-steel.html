Stainless Steel Motors & Gear Motors

The stainless steel (INOX) drives are particularly suitable for extremely moist applications. Whether it be conveyor systems, hygienic applications or intralogistic; the characteristics of the design of these drives ensure a perfect solution for:

  • Food & beverage Industry
  • Dairy Factories
  • Offshore Industry
  • Fishing Industry
  • Pharmaceutical Industry
