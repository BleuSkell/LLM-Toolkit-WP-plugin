Venanzetti Electric Vibration Motors

A vibratory motor is a three-phase motor that is intentionally unbalanced, and is also known as an eccentric rotating mass (ERM) or vibrating motor. Vibratory motors are used to vibrate sieves, troughs and tables, but also to separate products in a bunker or landfill pipe. Vibratory motors ensure an efficient and continuous process with an unobstructed flow of material. They handle everything from fine particles such as flour, sand and powders to coarser materials such as wood chips, gravel and coal.

BEGE’s Various Industrial Vibrators

Standard Vibration Motor

The standard range of electric vibration motors is suitable for all types of vibrating devices and most industries.

These vibratory motors are certified in accordance with ATEX II2D, Ex, tD A21 IP66 T120°C and CSA Standard C 22.2 n° 100-95, Class 4211 01, meaning they are approved for use in combustible dust environments by default.

The VV series provides from 20 to 26000 kg of centrifugal force with 2, 4, 6 or 8 poles. These electric vibration motors come with a 3-phase or 1-phase motor.

Micro Vibration Motor

Compact rotary electric vibration motors with low centrifugal force.

The VV Micro series is designed for small surfaces, light vibration and low centrifugal force; to extract materials from a silo, for small vibrating tables or to make a product vibrate. Micro vibration motors are especially well-suited to the food and packaging sectors.

These micro vibration motors are certified in accordance with ATEX II3D, Ex tc IIIC T100°C Dc IP65, Zone 22, meaning they are approved for use in combustible dust environments in Europe by default.

ATEX Vibration Motor

Explosion-proof industrial vibration motors for the European market with special features and strict tolerances to prevent possible explosions within the motor from affecting the external surroundings.

ATEX VVE vibratory motors come with a 3-phase or 1-phase motor. They are certified compliant with ATEX II2GD, Ex e IIC T3/T4 Gb, Ex tb IIIC T120°C.

This makes them eminently suited to applications with strict safety regulations such as in the oil and gas industry or other explosive environments (gas and dust).

CSA Vibration Motor

Explosion-proof industrial vibrators for the North American market with special features and strict tolerances to prevent possible explosions within the motor from affecting the external surroundings.

CSA VVC vibratory motors come with a 3-phase or 1-phase motor. They are certified compliant with CSA Standard C 22.2 n° 100-95, file n° 216103 Class 4228 01; Class I, Division 2, Groups A, B, C and D.

This makes them eminently suited to applications with strict safety regulations such as in the oil and gas industry or other explosive environments (gas and dust).
