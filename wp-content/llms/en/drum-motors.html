LAT drum motors

A drum motor is a completely enclosed electric drive, with the electric motor, gear box and bearings located in the drive drum. Thanks to this construction with the gear motor integrated in the drum, a drum motor has a very compact design and all internal components are protected against external factors. The drum motor transfers the required power to a conveyor belt, converting the drum torque into traction force. A guide drum is a drum without an electric motor, which tightens the belt to create traction on the drive drum. Drum motors are also used in beltless drives.

Industrial drum motors

A conventional drive is located on the side of the machine, for example a belt conveyor. This arrangement regularly leads to damage to the gear motor, for example if heavy items such as stones fall off the conveyor in a cement plant. There is also a risk of a forklift truck accidentally colliding with the installation and damaging the gear motor.

Unlike a conventional drive, a drum motor does not have any external components, so it can be integrated into the belt conveyor and takes up less space. This greatly reduces the risk of damage.

Applications of LAT drum motors

For decades, LAT drum motors from Himmel Technologies have been the ideal drive solution for installations in numerous industries. Due to the compact and fully enclosed drive unit, drum motors are used in the most extreme conditions. Here we describe drum motor applications in more detail in three industries: the food industry, packaging & logistics, and bulk materials.

Food & Beverages

LAT drum motors are the ideal drive for food processing and food production applications. Thanks to the encapsulated stainless steel construction, drum motors excel in hygiene. By using only premium food-safe materials such as stainless steel and special no-tox oil, our drum motors meet the highest possible hygiene standards.

Belt conveyors with an integrated drum motor run more efficiently than with a conventional drive, leading to higher productivity. LAT drum motors are very accurate and can start and stop quickly. All components are integrated in the drum motor, so it takes up less space. Finally, our drum motors are plug-and-play units for easy installation, saving time and money.

Materials

  • FDA approved cladding
  • Profile suitable for modular (link) belts including Intralox, Ammeraal, Habasit, Unichain MPB, Scanbelt, Cleandrive and Thermodrive.

Advantages

  • Fully enclosed stainless steel housing (IP69K)
  • No external gear reducer
  • Compliant with hygiene standards
  • No dust or dirt accumulation
  • Easy cleaning with steam and high pressure

Packaging & Logistics

Drum motors are mainly used in the packaging and logistics sector to transport, push, feed and discharge goods. For horizontal transport as well as moving to other levels.

Drum motors are also used in dynamic warehouses where high-throughput products are processed quickly and maximum benefit is achieved in minimum space. Pallet flows and carton flows help to significantly reduce order picking time.

Materials

  • Possible with an encoder. This makes the drum motor the ideal solution for use in dynamic conveyors. An encoder enables positioning.
  • Possible with a brake and return lock. With a brake and return lock, the drum motor is perfectly suited to non-horizontal conveyor belts because the product does not roll back.

Advantages

  • Dynamic feedback with encoder
  • Options for non-horizontal conveyors
  • Compact design
  • Long life and low maintenance
  • Suitable for S1-100%

Bulk & Solids

More and more goods need to be collected and processed separately and on time in global material flows. Powerful logistics and an efficient system are vital, and LAT drum motors are an essential part of efficient and safe transport of bulk goods.

Increasing efficiency, increasing reliability and reducing costs are essential elements.

Space-saving drum motor mounting directly in the conveyor frame maximizes transport capacity with the same surface area. A positive side effect of this is that the mass is evenly distributed over the elegantly designed belt conveyors.

Design

LAT drum motors are well protected against dust thanks to the internal gear box. With no external components, the design provides high protection against external factors. This reduces the risk of damage and extends the service life.

Advantages

  • No external gear box
  • Well protected against dust and dirt
  • Protected against external factors
  • Uniform distribution of bulk goods
  • Space-saving design
