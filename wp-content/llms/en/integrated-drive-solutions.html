Drive partner

We’ve been excelling in drives for 70 years. We have years of experience in supplying technical components and package solutions to machine builders at home and abroad.

We provide added value with tailored solutions, instead of standard solutions that don’t fit. We build long-term relationships through which we focus on the optimal contribution of our drive systems to realizing our customer’s specific ambitions.

BEGE offers advice, guidance and delivery of innovative, customized solutions for power transmission. We ensure that the drive systems we provide seamlessly fit with our customer’s business processes.
