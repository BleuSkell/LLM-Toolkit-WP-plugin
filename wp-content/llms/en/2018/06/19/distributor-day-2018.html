Distributor Day 2018

  • 19 June 2018

This week BEGE organized our second Distributor Day.

This yearly event revolves around the “BEGE” family, the network of BEGE and our valued partners.

Cooperation

Our distributor network is instrumental to our ambition to become the best support partner. We consider distributors to be an important link to customers across the border. To show our appreciation and support future cooperation, we organized a second Distributor Day in June of 2018.

Interactivity

A dozen or so distributors from various European countries met at the BEGE headquarters in Sassenheim for a day of meetings, sharing of information and conviviality.
This interactive day consisted of a mix of presentations, networking, product innovation, exchanging experiences and a tour and ended with some nice drinks and dinner.
