BEGE will attend at the Hannover Messe

  • 8 March 2018

For the first time BEGE will be present at the Hannover Messe that takes place at the end of April 2018.

The Hannover Messe is one of the most important events for industrial technology and will be this year an inspirational meeting place for more than 5000 exhibitors and 220,000 highly qualified international visitors.

Integrated Industry – Connect & Collaborate

For five days the industry will show its latest industrial technologies and innovations. Visitors are informed about the interplay between automation and energy technology, intralogistics, IT platforms and artificial intelligence with which the digital transformation drives the industry forward. With the lead theme “Integrated Industry – Connect & Collaborate”, Hannover Messe 2018 spotlights the potential of this development

BEGE at Pump Plaza

You will find BEGE in hall 15, at Pump Plaza. This square is the center of expertise for manufacturing, research, development and application of pumps and pump systems during the fair. BEGE presents the new standard in industrial automation with the innovative MIG pulse generator and Stainless steel economy engines. Discover how our integral drive systems empower your business.

Free entry ticket

As an exhibitor we offer you the opportunity to visit the Hannover Messe for free. You can register for a free entrance ticket via the button below. You save € 84 on a multi-day ticket. Our special promotion code is: rcbys.
