BEGE present at WoTS and Engineering in F&B

  • 17 October 2018

In recent weeks, BEGE has been present at the World of Technology & Science as well as Engineering in Food&Beverage.

De afgelopen weken was BEGE aanwezig op zowel World of Technology & Science als Engineering in Food & Beverage.
Wij kijken terug op twee succesvolle evenementen waarbij we veel relaties gesproken hebben en boeiende gesprekken voerden.

Successful trade fairs

We can look back on two successful events, at which we met with a great many business contacts and had very interesting discussions. Our goal at both trade fairs was to inform visitors about how our integrated, customer-specific drive solutions can enable them to achieve production facilities and machines that are hygienic as well as efficient. We demonstrated how, with expert advice and professional expertise, we integrate separate components into complete and optimized drive systems that greatly reduce their company’s workload.

High-quality components

Prominent positions on our trade fair stands were reserved for our MIG encoder and stainless steel drives and electric motors. Interested parties had the opportunity to explore the features and possibilities of these high-quality drive components that enable drive units to operate as optimally as possible in terms of hygiene and efficiency.
