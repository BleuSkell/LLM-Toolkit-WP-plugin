Looking Back on SPS

  • 5 December 2018
Home » News » Looking Back on SPS

We look back on our presence at SPS IPC Drives 2018.

Last week, BEGE was present in Nuremberg, Germany for the three-day trade fair SPS IPC Drives. We look back on a very successful event, thanks in part to our European distributors.

Integrated Drive Solutions

The main focus of our participation this year was informing visitors of our integrated customer-specific drive solutions. We demonstrated how, with expert advice and professional expertise, we integrate separate components into complete and optimized drive systems, greatly reducing companies’ workloads.

High-Quality Components

Prominent positions on our trade fair stands were reserved for our MIG encoder and stainless steel drives and electric motors. Visitors had the opportunity to explore the features and possibilities of these high-quality drive components that enable drive units to operate with optimal hygiene and efficiency.

Finally, we’d like to thank everyone – visitors, business relations, prospective customers and partners – for their positive reactions to our new look, website and general professionalization.
