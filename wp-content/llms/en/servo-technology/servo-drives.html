Precision servo drives

For optimal drive systems, we offer precise and accurate servo drives from LS Electric.

LS Electric servo drives

Each type of servo drive has its own field of application and supports different types of feedback.

LS Electric servo drives can be configured using LS Electric’s free software. By adding a PLC or motion controller to the system, it is possible to program and control more complex movements.

Our servo drives, with power from 0.75 to 15kW, combined with a servo motor, gear unit and motion controller, form a complete servo drive system from a single supplier.

Servo drive features and benefits:

  • Highly efficient
  • Cost effective
  • Powerful
  • Real-time monitoring
  • Support various motor and encoder drive systems
  • Improved control performance
  • Full closed-loop control
  • Support position control mode
  • Excellent system implementation at a competitive cost
  • Easy to use

BEGE servo drives: the different ranges

Our product portfolio consists of six powerful and precise servo drives.

Integrated servo system – PEGA

This servo system with integrated improved efficiency is a combined system of motor, encoder and servo drive. It maximizes the usable space when installed in confined and small spaces.

View the PEGA series

Low voltage DC drive – PHOX

The PHOX series servo drive is a compact high-performance low-voltage drive (< 60 V DC). This direct drive is suitable for small applications and is ideal for movement in confined spaces.

View the PHOX series

Compact single axis servo drive – SubG

With its high-performance drive control and servo functionality, SubG is the right choice for a wide range of applications. SubG is suitable for almost all environments and applications.

View the SubG series

3-phase servo drive – SubD

The 3-phase servo drive SubD is characterized by precise positioning, excellent reliability and high efficiency for an optimal price. The drive is specially developed for industrial machines.

View the SubD series

Multi-axis servo drive – SubMP

The multi-axis servo drive SubMP is a powerful all-rounder and enables the construction of compact, cost-efficient and energy-saving machines with a high energy density.

View the SubMP series

Economy-type servo drive – SubAC

The economy-type SubAC servo drive is a cost-effective IEC/UL compliant drive. It offers stable and reliable performance in full closed-loop mode and offers optimal machine management.

View the SubAC series

Low voltage DC drive – PHOX

This low voltage DC drive enables real-time control via EtherCAT for DC automation systems. Variable switching frequency and full closed-loop control ensure very precise control.

View the PHOX series

All-in-one EtherCAT communications type – L7NH

Servo amplifier with EtherCAT connectivity. The L7NH series of servo drives provide the complete solution for controlling LS servo motors.

View the L7NH series

Servo drives – index function type – L7P

Servo amplifier with built-in positioning (indexing) module. These integrated control systems for position, speed and torque are easy to use and cost-effective.

This series offers a variety of functions such as backup, network diagnostics and monitoring, and includes built-in functions to improve efficiency.

View the L7P series

All-in-one EtherCAT fully closed-loop – L7NHF

The L7NHF series is the perfect solution for controlling LS servo motors. These integrated control systems for position, speed and torque are easy to use and cost-effective.

They feature full closed-loop control for ultimate precision.

View the L7NHF series

Pulse/analog function type – L7C

This integrated control system for position, speed and torque is easy to use and cost-effective.

L7C is a good choice for single-phase servo drive systems: a reliable and economical drive.

View the L7C series
