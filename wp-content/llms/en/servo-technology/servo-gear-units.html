Servo gear units

Special servo gear units have been developed for highly dynamic servo applications. Like all other gear units, servo gear units convert torque and speed ratios and are an essential part of a servo drive system.

Due to the high torsional stiffness at very low to constant rotation clearance, a servo gearbox enables extremely high acceleration and high torque. Servo gear units are therefore indispensable in applications requiring high positioning precision, short cycle times, precise speeds, high processing rates and maximum dynamics.

Servo gear units are very suitable for matching the inertia of the load to that of the motor in order to keep the control system stable.

We supply a wide range of servo gear units from the highly reputable Atek and LS Electric brands. These high quality gear units are very precise and are interchangeable with virtually all brands of servo motors.
