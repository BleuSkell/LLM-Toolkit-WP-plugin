HMIs

Human machine interfaces (HMIs) are panels that can be used to control and monitor the operational status of specific facilities and equipment.

BEGE supplies the XGT series from LS Electric. The panels are developed on Windows CE for easy use. They are also equipped with a clear and realistic display.

Thanks to rapid data transmission and processing, you benefit from an effective and cost-efficient control and monitoring solution.

HMI features and benefits:

  • Cost-effective control and monitoring solution
  • Graphical interface
  • Fast data transmission and processing
  • Easy job upload and download thanks to front USB port
  • Clear and realistic display
  • Modern, slimline and lightweight design
  • Extensive job memory
  • Free development and programming software
  • State-of-the-art graphics technology
  • Wide selection of communications interfaces

BEGE HMIs: the different ranges

Our product portfolio consists of three HMIs from LS Electric.

HMI standard – eXP

LS Electric’s eXP series HMIs provide effective control and monitoring solutions that are also cost effective.

These panels provide 30% better performance than comparable standard HMIs. They also offer a wider diversity of communications interfaces, which means they can communicate simultaneously with a PLC, barcode reader, VSD and temperature controller.

The eXP is 20% more compact in size and weight than the previous version, making it easier to fit into a control panel. This HMI is also available in IP66 and NEMA 4X.

View the eXP series

HMI premium – iXP2

Highly functional, user-friendly and robust HMI with responsive touch screen.

This robust HMI with IP66 aluminum frame and reinforced glass responds to multi-touch and gestures such as swipes and flicks.

The iXP2 can directly replace existing iXP HMIs. The dual-core CPU enables fast communications and processing. And thanks to multiple interface methods such as Ethernet 2ch, HDMI/audio output, USB host/device and multimedia, this panel is ready for the Internet of Things.

This HMI is also available in an IEC-Ex / ATEX version.

View the iXP2 series

HMI portable – iXP2H

A portable version of the iXP2 panel.

This HMI features the same functions and features as the iXP2 series and can also be used in applications where mobile control is required.

This handy model has a user-friendly 3-position switch and a bright display, and job transfer is easy thanks to the USB port and Ethernet connection.

View the iXP2H series
