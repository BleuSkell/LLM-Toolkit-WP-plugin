Coen Paulides new general manager at BEGE Power Transmission

  • 27 January 2023
  • Main news

BEGE announces management change

As of January 2023, Gerard Paulides will stop his active role in the management of BEGE Power Transmission. He hands over the management of the 72-year-old family business to his son Coen Paulides. The drive specialist also announces two other board members.

New management team

From January 2023, BEGE Power Transmission BV will appoint Coen Paulides as the new general manager. He succeeds the current director Gerard Paulides. On the same date, the management team is expanded with Alex van den Berg (Commercial Director) and Marcel Biesma (Operational Director).

From 2023, these three gentlemen will continue to lead the company, based on the existing company values and the jointly developed strategy. In the future, they will focus on themes such as customer-specific product development and digital automation. Gerard Paulides will remain involved as an advisor within the organization.

Generation change

The generation change already started with the arrival of Coen Paulides as International Sales Manager in 2016. In recent years, his involvement in strategic decision-making, product engineering and personnel development has gradually increased.

Mr. Paulides’ broad technical knowledge, his understanding of the changing drive market and his desire for a positive corporate culture make him the suitable general manager of the 72-year-old family business.

Coen Paulides, new general manager

Coen Paulides: “As the 3rd generation within our family business, I strive to continue to make a valuable contribution to the Dutch and international automation industry.

Providing innovative solutions to make industrial processes more productive, efficient and reliable is my top priority. In addition, being a good employer is an important spearhead and we will continue to invest in our colleagues and encourage personal growth.”
